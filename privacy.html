Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how WAHH AIR handles information when users access the WAHH AIR website, management portal and connected social media features, including integrations with platforms such as TikTok.

1. Information we may collect

Depending on the features used, WAHH AIR may process account profile information, business contact information, social media account identifiers, authorised profile information provided by a connected platform, content drafts, captions, media references, publishing status, scheduling information, access tokens, refresh tokens and technical logs required to operate the service.

2. TikTok and other connected platform data

When a user connects TikTok or another supported platform, WAHH AIR only requests information and permissions required for the features selected by the user. Connected platform credentials such as access tokens are handled server-side and are not intentionally exposed in public frontend code. WAHH AIR does not ask for or store the user's TikTok password.

3. How information is used

Information may be used to authenticate authorised users, connect social accounts, prepare approved social media content, schedule or publish owner-approved posts, display account connection status, maintain task and publishing history, diagnose failures, protect the service and comply with platform requirements.

4. AI-assisted features

WAHH AIR may use AI services to assist with internal business tasks such as drafting captions, campaign ideas or social media plans. Only information considered necessary for the requested task should be sent to the relevant provider. AI-generated content is treated as a draft unless separately approved for publication.

5. Sharing of information

WAHH AIR does not sell personal information to advertisers. Information may be processed by service providers that are necessary to operate the application, such as hosting, database, AI and connected social media platforms. Information is shared only as required to provide the requested functionality, maintain security or meet legal obligations.

6. Data retention

Information is retained only for as long as reasonably required for business operations, account connection, publishing history, audit, security or legal purposes. Tokens may be refreshed, replaced or removed when an account is disconnected or access is revoked.

7. Security

WAHH AIR uses reasonable technical and organisational safeguards, including server-side handling of private API credentials and access controls for administrative features. No internet service can guarantee absolute security, so users should also protect their own accounts and devices.

8. User choices and rights

Users may request correction or deletion of applicable information held by WAHH AIR, subject to operational, audit or legal requirements. Users may revoke access to a connected third-party platform through that platform's account settings where supported and may contact WAHH AIR to request disconnection of an integration.

9. Children

The WAHH AIR business management and social media administration features are not intended for children and are designed for authorised business users.

10. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when WAHH AIR features, providers or applicable requirements change. The latest revision date is shown above.

11. Contact

Privacy questions, access requests or deletion requests may be directed to WAHH AIR through www.wahhair.com or by phone at 011-16413800.
